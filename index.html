Coronado
Mexican Kitchen & Margarita House

As marketing coordinator for Coronado, I managed the restaurant's website through Popmenu, including the homepage, menu, catering, and ordering pages. I also planned and sent automated email and text campaigns and tracked and responded to guest reviews across Google and the website, where we maintained 82% positive sentiment across 57 reviews. One project I especially enjoyed was redesigning the kids' menu. I turned the original generic activity sheet, "Young Amigos," into a themed "Royal Amigos" storybook experience. I also created an original guest survey to better understand what actually brought people into the restaurant, including awareness of the restaurant's food-truck brand, Inches Tacos, as well as visit frequency and ordering habits. Biokoa
Organic Chocolate Bar

As part of the social media team for Biokoa, a small Colombian organic cacao brand, we built a content strategy centered on the creation of meaningful content to its audience (women and men in their early 40's) while reaching a new potential niche (healthy young audience) by pairing the brand's chocolate bars with everyday recipes, from fruit with cocoa cream to vegan chocolate ice cream. The goal was to give followers something useful and engaging, rather than just promoting the product. By keeping the strategy organized around a clear narrative, we were able to give the brand a more consistent direction and grow the account to 200+ followers across 19 posts within its first six months.

Per Tutti

Per Tutti was a student-run volunteer organization that published legal education and current affairs content on Instagram, completely outside of coursework. As a member of the Design Committee, and an occasional contributor to research, I designed weekly Instagram posts that turned legal, political, and economic topics into content that was easier to understand and visually engaging, while following the group's brand guidelines. The topics ranged from practical legal rights and civic participation to economic policy, public health, and pop culture. As a group, we grew the account to 2.3K followers by combining research with design and covering topics that gave us the opportunity to interview leaders and professionals, including our coverage of the 2022 Colombian presidential elections.

    Photography built around what your brand needs to say.

    PhGalleryZ is a personal photography brand I run alongside my marketing work. Through the different projects I've worked on, I've realized that although an image can be worth a thousand words, without purpose or context, it can lose a lot of its potential. That's why I don't approach photography as simply taking a set of pretty pictures. I start by asking what the brand is trying to communicate and getting a sense of its niche and target audience. From there, I build a visual narrative around it, thinking about things like mood, pacing, color, and composition to make sure the images are saying something rather than just looking good.

    Beyond the Plate: How Customer Motivations Shape Brand Communications

    When I first started working at Coronado, I didn't start in a marketing role. I started as a server, interacting firsthand with customers, hearing their stories, and getting to know more about who they were, what they did, and what they had to say. That naturally made me curious about why they chose our restaurant. Whether it was for a big event, a date, or even a solo meal, I wanted to understand what they valued about the experience.

    As time went by and sales started to slow, I felt like something was missing. Based on my experience with Biokoa and the importance of having a strong online presence, I looked at the restaurant's Instagram and noticed that it lacked engaging content and well-composed photography. But rather than immediately creating posts without context, I wanted to understand our customers first. Based on the different stories I had heard from them, I proposed including a survey with receipts so we could get a better picture of our guests and identify where a potential campaign could make an impact.

    I also proposed a drink photoshoot to give the page a stronger visual identity. After gathering insights, I noticed that people were missing a stronger sense of connection with the restaurant's culture and that our target audience were mostly families who put their kids first when choosing places to dine in. From there, I developed a narrative around repositioning how people saw the restaurant, drawing on its name to create a story-driven experience for families and kids through "Royal Amigos." I also proposed physical props for the restaurant to bring that world beyond the menu, although that idea ultimately didn't move forward because of budget constraints.
